# 07. User Guide

> Recruitment prototype. Use fictional data only.

## Citizens
1. **Register** (name, email, 10-digit mobile, password) or **Login**.
2. **Submit grievance**: choose district and category, enter subject and description. Optionally click **Get AI suggestions** to see a suggested department, category, priority and summary (you may ignore them). Attach up to 3 JPG/PNG/PDF files and submit.
3. Note your **reference ID** (e.g. `CMK-2026-483920`).
4. **Track**: use *Track Grievance* (no login needed) for status, department and history, or open your **dashboard** for pending/resolved counts and full details.
5. When a grievance is **Resolved**, read the department's action report, give a star rating and comment, or **Reopen** it with a reason.

## CMO administrators
1. Login as the CMO admin; the **dashboard** shows total/pending/resolved/overdue counts, district and department charts, and the full list.
2. Filter by district, category, priority, status or overdue. Escalated and overdue items stand out.
3. Open a grievance: read details and attachments, review the **AI suggestion** (labelled if rule-based) and any duplicate flag.
4. **Start review**, adjust priority if needed, then **Forward** to a department (optionally a specific officer). The AI suggestion is only a hint.
5. Add **internal remarks** (never shown to citizens) and **Escalate** delayed items.
6. When the officer submits a report, **Approve** (Resolved) or **Return** with remarks.

## Department officers
1. Login with the credentials issued to you (e.g. `officer.pwd@demo.test`).
2. The dashboard lists grievances forwarded to your department.
3. Open one, review details and attachments, **Post update** to record progress.
4. Write the **action-taken report**, **Save**, then **Submit to CMO for review**.
5. If the CMO returns it, continue and resubmit.

## Notes
- Status changes are recorded with time and the responsible user.
- Only the CMO can mark a grievance Resolved; only the citizen can reopen it.
